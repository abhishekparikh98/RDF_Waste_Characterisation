PART D – Budget Estimation & Financial Strategy (SPEAK THIS)

“Part D focuses on the budget estimation and
 financial strategy for the complete IT modernization
 of the City Health Clinic.”

“The total capital expenditure for this
 project is £24,860, which covers networking equipment, 
servers, installation, and contingency planning.”

“The Cisco 2911 router, costing £1,050, is used as
 the core gateway and inter-VLAN router to manage 
secure communication between departments.”

“Five Cisco 2960-X managed switches, costing £4,750,
 are used to support the distribution and access layers of the network.”

“Two wireless access points, costing £760, are deployed
 to support mobility in emergency and outpatient departments.”

“The most significant cost is the PACS imaging server,
 priced at £15,500, which is required for storing and
 processing high-resolution radiology images using the DICOM standard.”

“Additional costs include Cat6 cabling and manpower, 
totalling £2,800, to support installation across three floors.”

“A 15% contingency buffer of £1,162 is included
 to handle unexpected costs or price fluctuations.”

“To reduce expenses, the project uses certified refurbished equipment,
 resulting in approximately 30% cost savings without compromising reliability.”

“The implementation is phased, with critical clinical systems
 prioritised first, ensuring minimal disruption to hospital operations.”

“Overall, this financial strategy delivers a cost-effective, scalable,
 and future-ready infrastructure that supports patient safety and operational efficiency.”



2 Budget Withdrawal Contingency
In the event the issues of budget constraints occur, 
a Phased Implementation Strategy will be implemented.
•	Phase 1 (Critical): Deployment of the Core Router and Radiology Server
         to meet the regulatory standards and diagnostic imaging needs.
•	Phase 2 (Deferred): The work on delivery administration and network
        outpatient will be postponed until further funding can realize itself.
